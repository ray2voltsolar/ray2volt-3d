How it works

Three steps, one link

You keep the design and the client. We turn your layout into a plant your client can explore.

Start with the form
  1. 01

    Send your inputs

    Fill in the quick form for your fixed price. Then send the layout, equipment list, location and photos.

  2. 02

    We build it in 3–5 working days

    For Basic, or 2–3 for Pro, counted from the day we have complete inputs. A sketch may need one round of questions.

  3. 03

    Share one link with your client

    It opens in the browser on a phone or laptop. Nothing to install.

What we need from you

Five inputs from your team

The turnaround starts once we have inputs 1–4, plus input 5 if your client wants their name and logo shown.

  • 01

    PV layout

    AutoCAD/DWG is best. We also accept:

    • PDF drawing
    • PVsyst export
    • HelioScope export
    • Hand sketch

    A sketch must be clear, with the roof dimensions and the panel rows. It may need one round of clarification.

  • 02

    Site location

    A Google Maps pin, a KML file or the address.

  • 03

    Equipment and capacity

    Module and inverter models and counts, and the DC/AC capacity.

  • 04

    Site photos

    Phone photos are fine.

  • 05

    Client name and logo

    Only if your client wants them shown in the viewer.

Not offered: PV layout/design from scratch. A roof and a kWp target alone are not enough. Send the layout your team has made, and we turn it into 3D.

Optional: your string plan, SLD and design notes, if you want them shown in the viewer.

What the viewer includes

More than a pretty render

Six features come with every viewer. Two more show your own design documents, so they are added when you send them. Both live demos have all eight.

In every viewer

  • Your plant, in 3D

    The building, the roofs and every module row from your layout, with the site around it.

  • Orbit, zoom and tap

    Drag to orbit and pinch or scroll to zoom. Tap a module or inverter for its details from your equipment list.

  • One-tap camera views

    Jump to views such as the overview, the plan, the inverters and the grid connection.

  • Sun and shadows

    Pick a date and time, or play a whole day, and watch the shadows move across the array.

  • A guided tour

    A step-by-step tour that walks your client through the plant.

  • Made for phones

    A layout built for phone screens as well as laptops. Your client can also save any view as a picture.

Added when you send them

We show your design in 3D. We don't create it.

  • String plan and single-line diagram

    Your string plan and SLD, linked to the 3D model, plus your design notes.

  • Energy flow

    Animated power flow from the strings to the inverters and the grid, with hour-by-hour output estimates. Needs your string plan.

Energy figures in the viewer are model estimates (a clear-day curve and a typical-year total) to help your client understand the plant. They are not a guarantee of generation.

Who is Ray2Volt 3D for?

Solar EPCs and installers who sell commercial and industrial (C&I) projects. We work on C&I projects only.

What exactly do I get?

With Basic 3D: one hosted, interactive 3D web viewer of your project, on a link you send to your client. Pro 3D adds 45–60 s walkthrough videos made from the model (9:16 and 16:9, with captions) and carries your brand only.

The string plan, single-line diagram and energy flow are added when your design documents include them. We don't do the electrical design.

Does my client need to install an app?

No. The viewer opens in a web browser on a phone or a computer. Try a live demo on your own phone.

How fast is it ready?

Basic takes 3–5 working days and Pro 2–3, after we receive complete inputs. Proposal Desk subscribers get Basic in 3 working days. If something is missing or unclear, the clock starts once we have it.

We only have a hand-drawn sketch. Is that enough?

Yes, if it is clear and shows the roof dimensions and the panel rows. A sketch may need one round of clarification with you.

Can you design the PV layout for us?

No. We build the 3D viewer from your layout; we don't design layouts from scratch. A roof and a kWp target alone are not enough.

How is the price decided?

By the package and the total DC kWp of the plant. Basic starts at ₹4,999 and Pro at ₹9,999, + GST, up to 250 kWp; the price list shows every size. Plants above 3 MW, or with more than 5 separate roofs or blocks, are quoted. Your quote is fixed before work starts.

Is there a free sample?

No, but your first Basic viewer is half price: ₹2,499 + GST up to 250 kWp. It is one per company, checked by GSTIN, for a real C&I project.

We send proposals every month. Is there a plan for that?

Yes, two. A prepaid wallet: pay ₹50,000 and get ₹60,000 of work, or pay ₹1,00,000 and get ₹1,30,000 plus a priority queue, valid 12 months. Or the Proposal Desk subscription: ₹19,999 a month for 5 credits or ₹34,999 for 10, with white-label viewers and Basic in 3 working days.

How long does the link stay live?

12 months from delivery. After that, ₹999 per viewer per year keeps it live. Proposal Desk subscribers' viewers stay live while they are subscribed.

How do payment and changes work?

Basic is paid 100% in advance. Pro is paid 50% in advance and 50% before handover.

Our errors are always fixed free. Basic includes 1 round of changes within 30 days and Pro includes 2. Each extra round is ₹999 (Basic) or ₹1,999 (Pro).

Can I just message you on WhatsApp?

Yes, through the same form. Fill in the short enquiry form and tap Send on WhatsApp: WhatsApp opens with your details already in the message, and we reply there with your price.

Can the viewer show my client's name and logo?

Yes, if your client wants them shown. Send the name and logo with your other inputs.

Can you white-label the viewer with our company's branding?

Yes. Pro 3D viewers and every Proposal Desk subscription viewer carry your brand only. Basic viewers carry a small “3D by Ray2Volt 3D” credit.

Are the energy numbers in the viewer a guarantee?

No. They are model estimates (a clear-day curve and a typical-year total) that help your client understand the plant. They are not a generation guarantee.
