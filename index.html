What we need from you

Five inputs. Complete inputs start the clock.

The 3–5 working days start once we have inputs 1–4, plus input 5 if your client wants their name and logo shown.

  • Site location

    A Google Maps pin, a KML file or the address.

  • PV layout

    AutoCAD/DWG is best. A PDF, a PVsyst or HelioScope export, or a clear hand-drawn sketch with roof dimensions and panel rows also works.

    A sketch may need one round of clarification.

  • Equipment and capacity

    Module model and count, inverter model and count, and the DC/AC capacity.

  • Site photos

    Phone photos are fine.

  • Client name and logo

    Only if your client wants them shown in the viewer.

Optional: your string plan, SLD and design notes, if you want them shown in the viewer.

What we don't do

We don't design PV layouts from scratch. A roof and a kWp target alone are not enough. Send the layout your team has made, and we turn it into 3D.

What the viewer includes

More than a pretty render

Six features come with every viewer. Two more show your own design documents, so they are added when you send them. Both live demos have all eight.

In every viewer

  • Your plant, in 3D

    The building, the roofs and every module row from your layout, with the site around it.

  • Orbit, zoom and tap

    Drag to orbit and pinch or scroll to zoom. Tap a module or inverter for its details from your equipment list.

  • One-tap camera views

    Jump to views such as the overview, the plan, the inverters and the grid connection.

  • Sun and shadows

    Pick a date and time, or play a whole day, and watch the shadows move across the array.

  • A guided tour

    A step-by-step tour that walks your client through the plant.

  • Made for phones

    A layout built for phone screens as well as laptops. Your client can also save any view as a picture.

Added when you send them

We show your design in 3D. We don't create it.

  • String plan and single-line diagram

    Your string plan and SLD, linked to the 3D model, plus your design notes.

  • Energy flow

    Animated power flow from the strings to the inverters and the grid, with hour-by-hour output estimates. Needs your string plan.

Energy figures in the viewer are model estimates (a clear-day curve and a typical-year total) to help your client understand the plant. They are not a guarantee of generation.

FAQ

Questions EPCs ask

Who is Ray2Volt 3D for?

Solar EPCs and installers who sell commercial and industrial (C&I) projects. We work on C&I projects only.

What exactly do I get?

One hosted, interactive 3D web viewer of your project, on a link you send to your client. There is one product for now: no tiers or packages.

The string plan, single-line diagram and energy flow are added when your design documents include them. We don't do the electrical design.

Does my client need to install an app?

No. The viewer opens in a web browser on a phone or a computer. Try a live demo on your own phone.

How fast is it ready?

3–5 working days after we receive complete inputs. If something is missing or unclear, the clock starts once we have it.

We only have a hand-drawn sketch. Is that enough?

Yes, if it is clear and shows the roof dimensions and the panel rows. A sketch may need one round of clarification with you.

Can you design the PV layout for us?

No. We build the 3D viewer from your layout; we don't design layouts from scratch. A roof and a kWp target alone are not enough.

How is the price decided?

Prices start at ₹4,999. The price is fixed and depends on site size. Send us the site details on WhatsApp and we will tell you the price. We don't negotiate.

Can the viewer show my client's name and logo?

Yes, if your client wants them shown. Send the name and logo with your other inputs.

Can you white-label the viewer with our company's branding?

Not yet. White-label viewers are not offered at the moment.

Are the energy numbers in the viewer a guarantee?

No. They are model estimates (a clear-day curve and a typical-year total) that help your client understand the plant. They are not a generation guarantee.
